Welcome to 'A Legacy of Ashes'

Welcome to an interactive psychological drama where every choice you make alters the course of the story. You are the main character. Your decisions will determine whether you escape with your fortune, your family, or your life.


The 3 Rules of the Game

  1. Click to decide:Read the narrative text, the click on the linked text options at the bottom to make your move and turn the page

  2. Accept your fate:There's no 'undo' button mid-game. Once you click a choice, you must face the consequences of the path until you reach the ending.

  3. Explore All Paths:Reaching one ending isn't the end of the project. Use the 'Rewind Time' to restart from the beginning and discover how different choices completely change your destiny.
